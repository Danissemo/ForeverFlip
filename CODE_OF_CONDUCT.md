# Code of conduct

ForeverFlip is made by and for players. Everyone who takes part (in issues, discussions, pull
requests or reviews) is expected to keep it a place where people want to help.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, also when you disagree or a bug cost you gold.
- Stick to the facts: what you did, what you saw, what you expected.
- Assume good faith. Most reporters and contributors give their free time.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the addon, and that a "no" is not personal.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, and attacks on a person or a group of people.
- Posting someone's private information (real name, address, account details) without their consent.
- Spam, advertising, and selling or buying gold, items or accounts.
- Asking for, sharing or promoting ways to break the game's rules: bots, automation of clicks, or
  exploits. ForeverFlip only reads the auction house and never trades on its own, and it stays that way.

## Reporting

Report a problem with someone's behaviour to the maintainer,
[Danissemo](https://github.com/Danissemo), through a private message on GitHub, or use GitHub's
**Report content** on the comment. Reports are handled in confidence.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow
this code, in proportion to what happened: from a reminder to a permanent ban.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
