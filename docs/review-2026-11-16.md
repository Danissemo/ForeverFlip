## T31 rule (clarified 16 November)

- The requirement is fresh auction scan data, not a method. The timer is the newest of the end of
  the last complete Full scan (`scannedAt`) and the newest Sync scan the history accepted
  (`syncScanAt`). Running Sync without a newer scan moves nothing.
- No data at all: the warning says so and hides "You hold" and "Vendor pays" too.
